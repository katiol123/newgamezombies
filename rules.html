Rulebook · Level 1 “The Outskirts”

The first night after the city fell. Your squad is stuck on the outskirts. You must complete the objective and evacuate before the dead overrun the block. You play together against the game: either all the survivors escape, or the block wins.

The essentials in 30 seconds

Each round the heroes take turns spending 3 Energy ⚡ on cards and simple actions. Then the zombies move: they go for the nearest hero and attack. The cards left in your hand are your defense: you can discard them to block hits. Play cards of the same suit in a row to get the “Chain” bonus. Be careful with noise: noise raises the Alarm, and a full Alarm brings a Wave.

2Setup

  1. Board. Shuffle the tiles and lay out 8 random ones in a 4×2 grid (4 columns, 2 rows; in Scouting mode face down, see “Fog of War”), each in a random rotation (roll a die or just spin it without looking). The tiles are designed so that the middle of each side is passable, so any layout in any rotation is connected.
  2. Start. Pick a random tile in the left column and any zone on it at the left edge of the board (a street zone is better) — place the hero pawns there. The tile with the start is called the starting tile.
  3. Exit. Pick a random tile in the right column and place the exit token (car / ambulance / pickup) in any zone on it at the right edge of the board (a street zone is better). In “Last Broadcast” the helicopter lands differently — see below.
  4. Manholes. Solo — 2, two heroes — 3, three and four — 4. Place them on the manhole icons printed on the tiles: first on the tiles of the right column, then the middle, then the left. The starting tile and the exit tile are skipped (with 4 heroes the last manhole may end up on a left-column tile, next to the start). If a column has more tiles than needed, choose at random.
  5. Objective. Draw an objective card (or choose one).
    • Crates: take as many crate tokens as the objective card's “Setup” line says (it also says how many of them hold cargo) and shuffle them face down. Place them one at a time on different tiles in the same order as the manholes (right column first, the starting tile is skipped) — on a search icon inside a building, or, if there is none, on any search icon.
    • Tower (“Last Broadcast”) — in a building on a middle-column tile, preferably in the row without the start (if there are several buildings, the players choose). If no middle-column tile has a building — on a tile with a building in the right column, then the left (except the starting tile). In this objective the exit token (the helicopter) is placed not at the right edge but in the street zone closest to the tower.
    • Nests (“Burn the Nests”: solo 1, two heroes 2, three–four 3) — in the central zone of tiles, starting from the right column (if the centre is impassable — in an adjacent zone of the same tile).
  6. Search. Place search tokens on all remaining search icons.
  7. Condition and bonus. Draw a condition card and follow its instructions. Draw a bonus objective card — it gives the 3rd star.
  8. Decks. Each hero takes 6 shared and 4 of their own cards and shuffles them. Shuffle the loot and spawn decks.
  9. Tracks. The round marker goes on “1”. Alarm goes on “0”; a big squad (3–4 heroes) starts at “1”, and four heroes have a lower Wave threshold (4).
  10. The first dead. Draw a spawn card for each manhole (the “Dusk” row).
  11. Hands. Each hero immediately draws a full hand (up to their hand size). The “no more than 3 cards” limit applies only to the draw at the start of a turn.

3The round

0. Event (from round 2)Roll the die. On a “6”, draw an event card and resolve it — it lasts until the end of the round (unless stated otherwise). On 1–5 — all quiet. On average 1–2 events per game.
1. Hero turnsEach hero takes their turn. You choose the order — an important part of the tactics.
2. Zombie phaseMove → Attack → Special abilities → Spawn (and a Wave if the Alarm is full).
3. End of roundRemove lures, noise and “pinned”. Advance the round marker. If that was the last round — defeat.

The round track is split into times of night: Dusk (1–3), Night (4–6), Dead of Night (7–14). A squad of 3–4 heroes has more targets, so it gets 16 rounds. The darker it gets, the more dead on the spawn cards, and in the Dead of Night all zombies are hungry: +1 speed.

4A hero's turn

  1. Draw cards up to your hand size, but no more than 3 cards at a time. Hand size is the number on the hero board (each hero has their own: Boris and Lisa — 4, Marina, Viktor, Kira and Sava — 5, Gosha — 6) plus bonuses: solo “Loner” +1, “Backpack” +1, Boris's talent “Rock” +1; “Exhausted” −1. If you already hold at least the limit, draw nothing, but do not discard the excess either. If your deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile.
  2. Gain 3 Energy ⚡ (+ Adrenaline tokens, + 1 if you are “On the Edge”).
  3. Act in any order: play cards and take basic actions while you have ⚡.
  4. End your turn. Unspent ⚡ is lost. Cards in hand stay — you defend with them in the zombie phase.

Grip

Leaving a zone with zombies costs +1 ⚡ — both for the basic “Move” and for movement cards. You cannot pass through a zone with zombies — you must stop there. Climbing over a barricade also costs +1 ⚡. If there is a Grabber in the zone, leaving costs another +1 ⚡ (+2 in total). In stealth mode only hunters hold you — a wanderer has not noticed you yet.

5Cards and Chain

  1. Cost in Energy (the yellow circle).
  2. Suit (the icon on the right and the frame colour).
  3. Effect — what the card does.
  4. Chain — a bonus if the previous card played this turn was of the same suit.
  5. Shield (bottom left) — how much damage the card blocks if discarded during an attack.
  6. Noise — loud cards raise the Alarm.
Rage — strength and onslaughtAgility — movement and dexterityMind — tactics and support

Chain and Relay

You played “Strike” (Rage), then “Baseball Bat” (Rage) — the Bat's Chain triggers: +1 damage. Basic actions neither break nor continue a Chain — only cards count. Plan the order: sometimes it pays to play a weak card first to “power up” a strong one.
Relay: a hero's first card can continue the Chain from the last card of the previous hero this round. So the turn order is tactics too: pass the suit to one another.

🤝 Relations (campaign)

Each pair of heroes has a relation scale from −3 to +3: Enemies (−3), Dislike (−1…−2), neutral (0), Pals (+1…+2), Friends (+3).

They rise (+1): healing a comrade (including disinfecting and cauterizing a bite); a rescue — killing a zombie that bit a comrade in the last zombie phase; a mission completed together at 3 ★ (both survived). They fall (−1): a comrade was bitten by a zombie you woke or led in; you evacuated, leaving a comrade 1–2 zones from the exit; refusing to share a ration (a shelter event). Each reason counts for a pair at most once per mission; changes take effect after the mission.

Friends — Bond: if one ends their turn in the other's tile, the other plays their first card on their next turn with its Chain effect (if the card has one; if not, the bond gives nothing that turn). A friend's death — −2 morale. Pals give each other cards without the “once per turn” limit. Heroes with Dislike do not give each other cards. Enemies do not give cards either; whoever ends their turn in an enemy's tile takes 1 ⚡ from that enemy's next turn; if both are in the squad — −1 morale each after the mission; an enemy's death — +2 morale.

Special card types

A Consumable leaves the game after it is played. Gear stays in front of the hero and works permanently (no more than 2 — a third pushes the oldest into the discard pile). Two identical items do not stack: only one works. Cargo takes up a place in your hand; it cannot be played or discarded, but it can be given. A Wound is a dead card in your hand.

Loot. Found cards go straight into your hand, and after being played — into your discard pile: your deck grows and improves. The “Ambush!” card is revealed immediately: a Shambler (at night — a Runner) appears in your zone and the search is interrupted — choose only from the cards already drawn.

6Board, zones, line of fire

Each square of a tile is a zone. Streets are connected to each other; only doors (openings in the wall) lead indoors. Hatched zones (cars, trees, rubble) are impassable. A zone can hold any number of pieces.

Line of fire runs strictly in a straight line (row or column) through open passages. Walls and impassable zones block it, doors do not. Range is counted in zones: 0 — your own zone, 1 — an adjacent one, and so on.

Noise. Loud actions put noise tokens in the hero's zone and raise the Alarm by the same amount. Noise is removed at the end of the round.

🚪 Doors and cover

On every entrance to a building (an opening between a room and the street or another room) place a door token “closed” side up. Doors do not hinder moving or shooting.

Creak. A hero passing through a closed door opens it (flip the token) and puts a noise token in the zone on the other side of the room — the Alarm does not rise, but the noise attracts zombies. Zombies that run into a closed door spend one step to push it open (silently) — closed doors hold the crowd back.

Close doors (1 ⚡): close all doors of the room you are standing in.

Cover. If all doors of a room are closed and there are no noise tokens inside, the heroes in it are not visible to zombies more than 1 zone away — those do not target them. Zombies in an adjacent zone or in the room itself sense the heroes as usual. Any attack or noise inside gives away the cover until the end of the round. Noise outside (including a creak) attracts zombies: if there are no heroes there, they go to check. Reaching an empty noise, a zombie removes the token (“no one”) and uses its remaining steps to head for a new target.

Fog of War (Scouting) — the default mode

Setup with fog. Lay out the 8 tiles face down in a 4×2 grid. Flip one random tile in the left column — the starting tile — and place the heroes in any zone on it at the left edge of the board (a street zone is better). In the same way flip a random tile in the right column and place the exit in a zone on it at the right edge of the board. Flip tiles “away from you”, like a book page, without rotating. Place manholes, crates, the tower and nests by the usual rules, but on a closed tile — on its back: you can see what is there, but not where. In “Last Broadcast” the tower tile is flipped right away (the helicopter lands next to the tower).

Scouting. A hero in a zone at the edge of an open tile can use the basic “Move” action to step into an adjacent zone of a closed tile. Flip the tile “away from you” and move the tokens from its back: a manhole to a manhole icon, a crate to a search spot in a building (if there are several, the players choose), the tower into a building; place search tokens on the free search spots. If there turns out to be a wall between the zones, the hero stays put and the ⚡ is spent. You cannot enter the fog with movement cards.

Zombies in the fog and decoys. Zombies that appear on a closed tile (from a manhole, a Nest, events) are not placed right away: for each zombie on the spawn card, put 2 tokens face down on the tile back. For each, roll the die: 1–3 — that zombie's token, 4–6 — a token from the decoy bag. The stack does not move — no game master is needed. Everyone sees how many tokens there are, but what they really are you learn only when the tile is revealed: five tokens may turn out to be a horde or an empty yard (sometimes a whole spawn card produces no one). Nests, the Hound and turned heroes are always real (place them face up).

Revealing a tile. Flip the tokens in the stack. Place the zombies in the manhole (or Nest) zone. Decoys: “Gone” (17) — empty, they slipped away before you arrived (the token goes back into the bag; special decoys leave the game once revealed; if the bag is empty, place only zombie tokens); “Lair” (1) — put a find token on this spot (an armory search); “Ambush from above” (1) — draw a spawn card for the nearest unplugged manhole; “Tracks” (1) — the nearest zombie gets a trail to this zone and goes to check (without stealth — a noise token). If a stack reaches 8 tokens (checked in the “Special abilities” step), the tile opens by itself — a horde breakout.

You cannot shoot, place lures or traps through closed tiles, and zombies on open tiles do not path through the fog. The mode can be switched off during setup — then all tiles are open from the start.

🌙 Stealth — the default mode

Zombies do not know where you are. Each zombie is either wandering (a normal token; in the app — a “…” icon) or hunting (a “!” marker under it). A special kind of wanderer is lurking (in the app — a “z” icon: asleep until woken). New zombies appear wandering; they appear hunting if they spawn in a zone with a hero or during a horde frenzy (but not in the fog: a stack on a tile back is always “wandering”).

Spotted. Zombies “look around” at the end of each hero's turn and in the zombie phase (at the start and after moving) — the same for all. A wandering zombie spots a hero if the hero is: in its zone; in an adjacent zone (through any passage, even a closed door — it can hear breathing); or in a straight line along a row/column up to 4 zones through open passages — walls, closed doors and fog block such sight. A hero in cover is spotted only from an adjacent zone. The zombie that spots flips to “hunting” and puts a “?” trail marker on the hero's zone. So on your turn you can sneak up, strike and slip around a corner — if you are not visible by the end of your turn, you were not spotted. But noise and hits wake them at once (see “Heard”).

Heard. Noise is heard at once: 1 + the number of noise tokens zones from its source (a door creak — 1 zone, a Lure — 3). All zombies within this radius that cannot currently see heroes start hunting and head for the noise (trail — in the noise zone). Hitting a zombie also wakes it.

A hunter goes for the nearest visible hero (or Lure) by the usual rules; if it sees no one — to its last trail. A wanderer takes 1 step per phase: roll the die once for all of them — 1–2 towards the nearest manhole, 3–4 towards the centre of the board, 5–6 they stay. Wanderers do not attack.

Lurking. A special kind of wandering zombie (set by the mission: ambushes, lairs, sleeping bosses). A lurker takes no steps on the wanderers' die and does not attack — it just waits in place. It wakes up the same way as a wanderer: it spots a hero, hears noise or a hit, a horde frenzy (Wave) begins — or by a special mission rule. Once awake it immediately becomes a hunter and no longer lurks.

The third side — bandits (campaign missions). To a bandit both heroes and zombies are enemies, equally; zombies hunt bandits just like heroes. In a zone with both a hero and a bandit, a zombie picks its victim at random; a bandit picks whom to hit the same way. A bandit does not chase enemies: it hits an enemy in its zone or shoots the nearest one on a line up to 2 zones, otherwise it loots. The wanderers' die for a bandit: 1–4 — a step towards the nearest loot (a crate or a Search token), 5–6 — it stays. In a zone with loot, a bandit immediately takes it into its bag. Each bandit attack is Noise for 2 zones; to a hero it deals only a Wound (no Infection), to a zombie — 2 damage. A killed bandit drops its bag in its zone; one mauled by zombies rises as a Shambler. Special gang fighters (the sniper, the leader) are described on their cards and in the mission briefing.

Lost the trail. At the end of the zombie phase, a zombie that sees no one and has already reached its trail removes its markers and wanders again. A zombie that cannot reach its trail (for example, the fog cut it off) loses the trail too. To shake it off, running away is not enough: you need to go around a corner, behind a closed door or far away — and wait until the hunter checks the last trail.

Horde frenzy. When the Alarm reaches the threshold (a Wave), all zombies on the board start hunting and see all heroes at any distance — until the end of the next round (even lurking and in cover). Quieter means longer lives.

The mode can be switched off during setup — then all zombies always know where you are (classic).

7Combat and wounds

Mark damage to zombies with damage tokens: it lasts until the end of the round, so another hero can finish off a wounded zombie. At the end of the round remove the damage tokens. The exception is large targets (Brute, Nest): their damage stays until the end of the game. Sentries on post (bandits who, per the objective, stand in the exit zone or at the barrier) also accumulate wounds. Armor reduces each separate damage effect.

If an effect hits “everyone in the zone” or “one more”, the damage is distributed to kill as many as possible, starting with the most dangerous.

Defense

  1. The damage of all a hero's attackers is added up.
  2. First it is absorbed by Block tokens (from “Take Cover” etc.) and gear.
  3. Then you can discard cards from your hand: each blocks damage equal to its shield value. These cards go not to the discard pile but to your Fatigue pile.
  4. If damage got through, roll the die for each biting zombie (but no more than the damage): 1–2 — Infection (1 damage becomes an Infection), otherwise — a Wound. Gear and abilities change the Infection threshold, but it never goes below 1: a roll of “1” is always an Infection. All other damage is Wounds. A Wound goes into your discard pile (it will come to your hand later and take up space). Take an Infection card straight into your hand (it takes up a place in your hand when drawing).

😮‍💨 Fatigue

Cards discarded for defense lie in the Fatigue pile next to your discard pile. They do not go back into the deck when you reshuffle.

If there are 3 or more cards in Fatigue, the hero is exhausted: hand size −1, and all actions taken without playing a card from hand are blocked (move, search, punch, barricade, pick up, give, close doors, talent and gear actions) — except story ones (objective actions, rescuing a survivor, evacuation) and “Catch Breath”. Cards from hand, including movement cards, can be played. Careful: you cannot catch your breath in a zone with zombies — you will have to leave or clear the zone with cards.

Catch Breath (3 ⚡, in a zone without zombies) returns all cards from Fatigue to your discard pile. Sometimes it is better to take a Wound than to be left without strength.

☣ Infection and Turning

An Infection is also a Wound: it counts when checking whether you fall. It cannot be played.

If at the end of your turn an Infection card is still in your hand, the hero turns: flip their pawn — it is now a special monster (see “Enemies”), it moves and attacks by the zombie rules. Its cargo and 2 random cards from its deck stay in the zone — another hero can pick them up.

Rescue — before the end of your turn (you have one turn for it):

  • A Medkit removes Infections first, then Wounds.
  • Marina (1 ⚡, a hero in her zone, instead of healing a Wound) turns all their Infections into ordinary Wounds. Let her take her turn before the bitten hero!
  • Cauterize a bite (2 ⚡, yourself or a comrade in your zone, only in a zone without zombies) — Infections in hand → Wounds. Expensive, but it saves you — if you manage to break away from the enemies.

An Infection cannot be played, discarded for defense or given. Without a medkit and Marina, a bite in the thick of battle is almost certain death.

The safe and Valuables (some campaign missions)

A stack of 6 tokens is placed face down on the safe: 4 “Valuable”, 1 “Empty”, 1 “Siren” (shuffle them). Cracking is an action in the safe's zone without zombies: 2 ⚡ and Noise 2, reveal the top token. “Valuable” — take a Valuable card into your hand; “Empty” — nothing; “Siren” — an extra Noise 3, the safe jams: the remaining tokens leave the game, and the panicked safecracker drops one Valuable from hand (if any) — it flies into an adjacent zone (choose at random). Any hero can pick it up there with the usual action — even if there are zombies in that zone. You can crack as many times as you like while there are tokens and energy — everyone can see how many tokens are left: the longer you loot, the closer the siren.

A Valuable is cargo: it takes up a place in your hand, it cannot be played or discarded to block; it can be given. It jingles: while you hold a Valuable, each of your noisy actions is Noise +1 (several Valuables do not stack). In the campaign, after the mission a Valuable is sold to the fence — the price is unknown until the sale, from 2 to 8 🎫.

↩ Retreat (campaign)

A campaign gives you 2 retreats. On any of its turns the squad can decide to retreat: the mission is aborted, all heroes on the board return to the shelter (wounds stay), there are no rewards, all Valuables from this mission are abandoned, each squad member gets −1 morale, and the mission must be replayed. A failed mission (time ran out or everyone fell) also uses up a retreat and is replayed. When no retreats are left, a failed mission ends the campaign.

Falling

When a hero has as many Wounds and Infections (in the whole deck, hand and discard pile) as their Grit ♥, the hero falls: remove the pawn; their cargo and 2 random finds (loot cards, trophies, campaign cards, Valuables — not the hero's basic cards) stay in the zone, and a Shambler rises in their place. The same happens on turning. If a hero has exactly one Wound fewer than their Grit, they are “On the Edge”: +1 ⚡ at the start of the turn.

Healing removes Wound cards from the game (first from the hand, then from the discard pile and the deck). Ordinary healing does not remove an Infection — only a medkit, Marina and “Cauterize a bite” do.

8Zombie phase

In stealth mode (see “🌙 Stealth”): only hunters move and attack; wanderers take 1 step by the die and do not attack; empty noise by itself is not a target — it is “heard” (zombies get a trail to the noise zone).

  1. Move. Each zombie moves as many zones as its speed towards the nearest target — a hero, a lure or a zone with a noise token and no heroes (count steps along the shortest path). If several targets are equally near: a lure beats a hero, a hero beats empty noise; then — where there are more noise tokens; then — the hero with fewer Wounds. If there are several shortest paths, the players choose. The target is chosen once — at the start of the phase, and the zombie heads for it with all its steps, even if something changed on the way (for example, a cover door opened). It looks for a new target only on reaching an empty noise. On reaching its target, the zombie stops. “Pinned” zombies do not move. Move zombies step by step: first all take one step, then those with speed 2+ take a second, and so on (a Runner — 2 zones, at night everyone is 1 faster).
  2. Barricades. A zombie takes the shortest path, ignoring barricades (among equal paths it picks a clear one). Running into a barricade, it hits it (−durability equal to its damage, minimum 1) instead of stepping and stays in place until the end of the phase.
  3. Attack. Each zombie in a zone with a hero attacks once. The target is the hero with the fewest Wounds (Boris intercepts attacks in his zone). Invisible heroes are not attacked. Crowding: no more than 3 zombies bite one hero per phase — the most dangerous ones (with the most damage); the rest jostle around or go for the next hero in the zone.
  4. Special abilities. Horde: if there are 8+ zombies on the board (Nests and lurkers do not count) — Alarm +1. Screamers raise the Alarm, Nests spawn zombies.
  5. Spawn. For each manhole draw a spawn card and place the zombies from the row of the current time of night.

Alarm and Wave

If in the “Spawn” step the Alarm has reached the threshold (solo and two heroes — 5, three — 6, four — 4), subtract the threshold from the Alarm and draw one more card for each manhole. Make noise only when it is worth it.

A Tripwire explodes as soon as a zombie enters its zone: damage to each zombie there, noise 2.

Out of tokens? If you need to place a zombie and there are no such tokens left (Shamblers 16, Runners 6, Bloaters 4, Screamers 3, Brutes 2), instead all zombies of that type on the board take one extra step towards their target (no more than once per spawn card).

9Experience and talents

Experience (🎖) comes only from dangerous enemies — how much is shown on the token: Bloater, Plaguebearer, Grabber, Brute, Hound, Nest, turned heroes. Shamblers, Runners and Screamers come in crowds and give no experience. An opened crate also gives 1 🎖. Some unique hero cards give +1 🎖 for a chain — it is written in their chain effect. At 4 🎖 a hero reaches level 2, at 10 🎖 — level 3. At each level choose one of the two talents on the hero board — it lasts until the end of the game.

10Objectives, victory and defeat

Evacuation. When the goal is complete, the exit zone opens. A hero in it can evacuate (0 ⚡): they leave the board and are saved. Victory — all surviving heroes have evacuated. Defeat — all heroes have fallen or the last round has ended.

Stars: ★ — victory; ★ — no one fell; ★ — the bonus objective was completed (the card is drawn during setup).

For bonus objectives with a count (“Hunter”, “Scavenger”, “Teamwork”, “Not a Scratch”, “Big Game”) use the bonus counter on the track board (0–24): move the marker each time the stated event happens.

🂠 Secret missions

Setup. Shuffle the mission deck (common ×2, rare ×1). Each player secretly takes one card for their hero. If a card has a mark that cannot be fulfilled in this game (“needs an ally” in solo, “needs stealth” or “needs fog” when that mode is off), put it at the bottom of the deck and take a new one. Changing a mission: only right when you get it — at the start of your first turn, before any action, a hero may once put the mission at the bottom of the deck and take a new one. After that the mission stays with them until the end.

The race. Keep your mission secret and track progress on its card. Once you complete it, reveal the card immediately. Only the one who completes their mission first gets the reward — others who complete theirs later get nothing. So it pays to hide your mission: an ally may “steal” your goal (finish off that very Brute, make the needed search).

The Traitor (a rare card) gets their way by exposing comrades to bites from zombies they woke or lured with noise. It is revealed on completion — in the campaign this is not forgiven: the traitor's relations with each squad hero −2. The traitor still needs the squad's shared victory — this is a dirty trick, not a betrayal of the whole sortie.

Start of round: roll the die; on a 6 — an event card.

Hero turn: draw up to the limit (max. 3 cards) → 3 ⚡ → act → cards in hand = defense.

Chain: the same suit as the previous card → bonus. Relay: the first card of a turn continues the previous hero's suit.

Grip: leaving a zone with zombies +1 ⚡. You cannot pass through zombies.

Search: 1 ⚡, no zombies in the zone; draw 2 loot, keep 1.

Damage to zombies lasts until the end of the round (large targets — permanently).

Zombie phase: move → attack → abilities → spawn.

Stealth: wandering / hunting (“!”); a lurker stays put until woken. Spots: own or adjacent zone, a straight line up to 4 zones. Hears: 1 + noise zones. Sees no one — goes to the “?” trail; once there — wanders again. Wave — the horde sees everyone until the end of the next round.

Zombie target: the nearest hero, lure or noise token; tie → lure → hero → noise → fewer Wounds. A hero in cover (doors closed, no noise) is not visible beyond 1 zone, except from the same room.

Doors: a hero through a closed one — a creak (noise outside, no Alarm); a zombie spends 1 step to open it. Closing a room's doors — 1 ⚡.

Attack: no more than 3 zombies per hero; block → gear → discard shield cards (into Fatigue; 3+ — exhausted) → a die for each biter: 1–2 Infection (to hand), the rest — Wounds (to discard).

☣ An Infection in hand at the end of your turn — turning. Saved by: a medkit, Marina, “Cauterize a bite” (2 ⚡).

Fog: “Move” into a closed tile — flip it; spawning in the fog — 2 tokens per zombie, 50/50 decoys; 8+ tokens — a breakout.

Alarm ≥ threshold: a Wave — an extra spawn card per manhole. Horde: 8+ zombies on the board (excluding lurkers) — Alarm +1.

Dead of Night: zombies +1 speed.

Experience: 4 🎖 and 10 🎖 — a new talent.

On the Edge (Wounds = Grit − 1): +1 ⚡.

In the digital version: click a card to select it, then click a target on the board (for a card without a target — click it again or press Enter); right-click a card to enlarge it; E — end turn; H — hint; 1–9 — cards in hand; Esc — cancel.
